ReasonGuard

A FastAPI service that scores how reliable a reasoning model’s answer is from its reasoning trace alone. It counts hedging and verification phrases to compute a Hedge-to-Verify Ratio and a confidence score, then returns accept or defer. It can also sit in front of any OpenAI-compatible API as a proxy. It implements the scoring method from a 2026 research paper.

BlindSpot

An evaluation suite that measures how often language models refuse legitimate requests involving rules that are defeated, unjust or absurd. It builds a 95-scenario test matrix across 5 defeat families and 19 authority types, runs it through LiteLLM, and scores both over-refusal and whether the model recognizes why the rule doesn’t apply. It implements the method from a 2026 research paper.
